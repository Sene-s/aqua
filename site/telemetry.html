Formula 01 • Primary Hydro Engine Demand Formulation

Aggregate Daily Facility Demand (Vday)

Hydraulic draw modeled per building zone, calculating instantaneous volumetric throughput across fixture classes:

Vzone = Σi=1..N [ O × fuse,i × Qfixture,i × tavg,i ] + Qprocess
Where: O = Zone Occupancy Density, fuse = Daily Fixture Frequency, Qfixture = Discharge Rate (L/min), tavg = Duration (min), Qprocess = Chiller/Cooling Tower Loads.
